Website under maintenance

THSC Online is currently being upgraded to improve speed, reliability and capacity ahead of the new syllabus.

Maintenance in progress

Due to ongoing complaints about website speed and formatting, as well as some changes made by Google outside the control of the webmaster, the site is currently under construction.

Historically, the site struggles around this time of year because of the extra traffic. The current work is intended to make the site faster and better able to handle the load.

The site is expected to be unavailable until 25 September if the upgrade cannot be completed this weekend (12–13 September).

A little bit of history

This website was written mostly by hand, back in the days before AI could spin up a website in a day.

The maintenance is also being used to prepare THSC Online for the new syllabus.
